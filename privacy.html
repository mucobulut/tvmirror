Privacy Policy

TV Mirror Pro (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and Windows) · DigiNova Labs · Last updated: October 16, 2026

Summary

TV Mirror Pro does not have accounts and does not send your screen, sound, photos, videos or files to our servers. Mirroring and media casting happen directly between your device and your TV on your local network.

Screen, sound and media

When you start mirroring, the app captures your screen and (if you allow it) sound, encodes it on your device and sends it only to the TV you selected on your local network. Photos, videos and music you choose are served from your device to that TV only while you are casting. Nothing is recorded or stored by us.

Local network and Bluetooth

The app scans your local network (Bonjour/mDNS, SSDP and Google Cast discovery) and can use Bluetooth to find and connect to TVs. This information stays on your device and is used only to show and connect to your TVs.

TV remote

To control a Google TV / Android TV, the app creates a security certificate on your device and pairs with your TV using the code shown on the TV. The certificate is stored only on your device.

Web videos, IPTV and YouTube

Addresses you enter (web pages, M3U playlists, YouTube links) are opened by your device or passed to your TV so it can play them. We do not receive or store them.

Advertising (free iPhone, iPad and Android versions)

The free mobile versions show ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads. Where required, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice in the app settings. Ads are limited to family-friendly content. See how Google uses data from apps. Subscribers to Premium do not see ads.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by the App Store, Google Play or the Microsoft Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Windows and Mac

The Mac version contains no ads and no analytics. The Windows version may download a list of our other apps from diginova.tr to show in-app promotions; no personal data is sent.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.
