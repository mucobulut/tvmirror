Support

My TV doesn't appear

Make sure the TV is turned on (not in standby) and connected to the same Wi-Fi network. Tap Refresh, or use "Connect by IP" with the TV's IP address. On iPhone/iPad allow "Local Network" access in Settings › TV Mirror Pro.

Which TVs are supported?

Google TV, Android TV and TVs or dongles with Chromecast built-in can be mirrored directly. Apple TV works with the system AirPlay; other smart TVs can play media over DLNA.

There is no sound

Turn on "Sound" before starting. On iPhone/iPad, keep "iPhone sound" on; the sound of apps is sent automatically while mirroring. On Mac, allow Screen & System Audio Recording in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Remote asks for a code

Enter the 6-character code shown on the TV. Pairing is needed once per TV.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel in your App Store / Google Play account settings. Restore purchases from the Premium screen in the app.
